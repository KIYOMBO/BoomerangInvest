# Boomerang Investor ROI Estimator

## Overview

Boomerang Investor ROI Estimator is a mobile-friendly dashboard tool for potential and existing Boomerang investors.

Boomerang is a shared asset management company where investors contribute money toward income-generating vehicles used for corporate mobility services. Investors earn a percentage of the vehicle’s profit based on how much they invested into that vehicle or asset pool.

This app helps investors estimate their possible return on investment by entering an investment amount and viewing projected vehicle income, expenses, profit, and investor returns over a selected period.

## Purpose

The purpose of the app is to give investors a clear visual estimate of how their investment could perform.

Instead of manually explaining the numbers, the app allows users to enter a few details and instantly see:

- How much of the vehicle they are effectively investing in
- The projected revenue from trips
- The expected vehicle expenses
- The projected profit
- The investor’s estimated share of profit
- The estimated return over 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 12 months

## Core Concept

An investor enters the amount they want to invest.

The system compares that amount to the total vehicle value or investment target.

For example:

```text
Investor amount: R20,000
Vehicle value: R335,000

Investor percentage = R20,000 / R335,000
Investor percentage = 5.97%
